About Digital Krishi Officer

Digital Krishi Officer is an AI-powered agricultural advisory system designed to bridge the gap between farmers and expert agricultural knowledge. Our system provides instant, context-aware advice in multiple languages, with special focus on Malayalam.

Built with advanced language models and fine-tuned agricultural datasets, the system understands local farming practices, seasonal patterns, and regional challenges to provide personalized recommendations.
